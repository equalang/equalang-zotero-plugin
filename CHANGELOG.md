# Changelog

## 0.1.10 (2026-11-22)

- Selection translation now starts as a compact Translate bar below Zotero's highlight colors by default, keeping the popup narrow when you are only highlighting text. Hover over the bar briefly or click it to open the translation; you can still choose "Translate as soon as text is selected" in Settings.

## 0.1.9 (2026-11-21)

- Your Equalang credit history now names the paper: selections made in the reader show the paper's title, and so do title and abstract translations when you signed in through the browser. Only the title is sent: no file path, link, DOI, item key or library ID.

## 0.1.8 (2026-11-04)

- Selection translation: dictionary entries show sooner. When the source language is known, the dictionary is looked up without waiting for the translation; while the AI explanation is on its way, its place says "Loading…".
- Selection translation: selecting an inflected form says what it is beside its headword, for example "comparative of high".
- Selection translation: the sources line names every dictionary shown, including the one the pronunciations come from.
- Document translation: opening translations when they're done is now an option, off by default: "Open translations when they're done" in the confirm dialog (your choice is remembered), and the same option in Settings.
- The confirm dialog's spinner stops once the credits are worked out.
- "Settings…" is translated in the 23 interface languages where Zotero itself leaves it in English.

## 0.1.5 (2026-10-26)

First public release.

- Document translation: Right-click a PDF, Word, PowerPoint or Excel attachment to translate it; the translation keeps the layout and is attached to the original item; PDFs can also come back as a bilingual edition.
- Selection translation: Select text in the reader and the translation appears immediately in a popup; selecting a word first shows dictionary definitions and British/American phonetic transcriptions, followed by contextual translation and AI-generated example sentences.
- Title and abstract: Right-click a selected item to translate its title and abstract; translations appear below the original title and abstract.
- Sign in to your Equalang account in the browser, sharing credits with the website.
- Supports Zotero 7, 8, 9 and 10, on Windows and macOS.
