# Changelog

## 0.1.5 (2026-10-26)

First public release.

- Document translation: Right-click a PDF, Word, PowerPoint or Excel attachment to translate it; the translation keeps the layout and is attached to the original item; PDFs can also come back as a bilingual edition.
- Selection translation: Select text in the reader and the translation appears immediately in a popup; selecting a word first shows dictionary definitions and British/American phonetic transcriptions, followed by contextual translation and AI-generated example sentences.
- Title and abstract: Right-click a selected item to translate its title and abstract; translations appear below the original title and abstract.
- Sign in to your Equalang account in the browser, sharing credits with the website.
- Supports Zotero 7, 8, 9 and 10, on Windows and macOS.
